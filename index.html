The Day My Forever Began

Every love story has a beginning, but I never knew that one ordinary day would become the beginning of my favourite story.

24 October 2020. A date that would slowly become one of the most precious parts of my life. I didn’t know then how many memories we would create, how many smiles we would share, how many difficult days we would face, or how deeply someone could become a part of my world.

Somewhere between our first conversations, our little jokes, our silly moments, and the memories that only we understand, you became more than just someone I loved. You became the person my heart kept choosing.

And now, six years later, if I could go back to that very first day, knowing everything I know today, I would still choose to begin this story with you.

If life gave me a thousand beginnings, I would still want every single one of them to begin with you.

Six years later, and my heart still has a favourite person. It’s you.

01

The Beginning

24 October 2020

It started with two people, a little curiosity, and a feeling neither of us knew would grow into six years of memories. You came into my life, and slowly, without even realising it, you became one of my favourite reasons to smile.

Falling for You

The little things became everything. Your messages, your voice, our conversations, the way a simple moment with you could make an ordinary day feel special. I wasn’t just falling in love with you; I was falling in love with the world we were creating together.

03

Growing Together

Six years are not made of perfect days alone. There are days when life gets busy, moments when we misunderstand each other, and times when love asks us to be patient. Through it all, our story has taught me that love is not only a feeling; it is also a choice we make, again and again.

04

Our Little Universe

Somewhere along the way, we created a world of our own. Private jokes, familiar habits, unforgettable conversations, and memories that nobody else could understand quite like we do. No matter how ordinary those moments seemed, they became pieces of a life I will always treasure.

05

The Future I Dream Of

When I imagine the future, I don’t dream only of grand adventures or beautiful places. I dream of the little things: waking up beside you, sharing meals, laughing about nothing, supporting each other’s dreams, and finding comfort in each other’s presence after a long day.

06

Still Choosing You

24 October 2026 — six years of us

We are no longer the two people who began this journey in 2020. We have grown, changed, learned, and collected so many memories along the way. Yet here we are, with another chapter waiting to be written. My favourite part? Our story doesn’t have to end here. There is still so much life left for us to discover.

A Letter to My Future Husband

Open me

To my love, my best friend, my future husband,

If someone had told me on 24 October 2020 that I would one day be celebrating six years of loving you, I would never have imagined all the little moments that would bring us here.

Six years may sound like just a number to the world, but to me, it holds a thousand memories, countless emotions, little dreams, and a love that has become part of who I am.

When I think about our future, I imagine more than a wedding day. I imagine a home where we can both be ourselves, a life where we celebrate each other’s victories, and a relationship where even the hardest days feel a little lighter because we face them together.

I want to be the person who listens to you when the world feels heavy. The person who celebrates your smallest achievements as if they were the biggest. The person who stands beside you when life doesn’t go according to plan.

And I hope we always remember that loving each other is not just about the beautiful days. It’s about understanding each other on difficult days, communicating when things feel uncomfortable, apologising when we are wrong, and making sure neither of us ever feels alone in a relationship meant for two.

One day, I hope I can look at you across our home and smile at the life we built together. Maybe we will laugh about how young we were when this all began. Maybe we will look at our old photographs and wonder how six years turned into so many more.

Happy sixth anniversary, my love. You are part of my favourite memories, my present, and the future I dream of.

If I have the privilege of growing old with you, I hope I never stop making you feel loved, and I hope we never stop choosing each other.

Forever has always sounded like a beautiful word. With you, it feels like a beautiful possibility.

With all my love,
Your girl ❤️

If our love story were a book, six years would only be the first few chapters.
